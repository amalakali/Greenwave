GreenWave

Traffic Lights That Think Green 🚦

GreenWave is a smart traffic management system that detects traffic density and automatically adjusts traffic signal timing.

By giving more green time to roads with higher traffic, GreenWave helps reduce unnecessary waiting, fuel wastage and vehicle emissions.

📌 About the Project

GreenWave is an Arduino-based smart traffic signal concept. Four IR sensors represent four roads and detect the presence of vehicles. The Arduino compares the traffic density and provides longer green time to the road with greater traffic demand.
